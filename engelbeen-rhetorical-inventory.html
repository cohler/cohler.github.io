F2 Three-quantity definitional substitution ("airborne fraction").
"the 'airborne fraction' at 53%"

The label "airborne fraction" as used in IPCC assessments and by Engelbeen names three physically distinct quantities that are then used interchangeably.

SymbolDefinitionValue
ABFL IPCC Glossary definition. Cumulative anthropogenic mass still airborne / cumulative anthropogenic mass ever emitted. The retention fraction. ≈ 6% under proper mass-balance reservoir routing with τ_res ≈ 3.8 yr
ABFC Ratio of contemporaneous rates. Annual atmospheric growth / annual anthropogenic emission. This is what is actually computed and reported as "≈ 44%" or "≈ 53%." Mean 42.7%, range 17.0%–74.1%, CV 29.1% over 1960–2025. Highly variable.
ABFS Smoothed rate ratio. ABFC low-pass filtered with an undeclared window of order 15–30 years. This is what "approximately constant" actually describes. Appears "stable" only because the smoothing window exceeds interannual variability.

The construction "the airborne fraction is stable at ≈ 44%, so roughly half of anthropogenic emissions remain airborne" computes ABFS, interprets it as ABFL, and licenses the interpretation with the Glossary label. The three quantities are not equal. ABFL and ABFC differ by roughly an order of magnitude under proper reservoir routing. Any argument that treats them as interchangeable is a definitional substitution, not physics.

F3 Accounting-identity fallacy ("below 100% proves it").

Operator-Equivalence Theorem (OET)

Setting. Let A be the linear exchange operator of the coupled atmosphere–reservoir carbon system, with eigenvalues {λⱼ}, right eigenvectors {vⱼ}, and left eigenvectors {wⱼ} satisfying wⱼᵀvₖ = δⱼₖ. Suppose the atmospheric perturbation is linear in the operator (empirically confirmed by the observed single-exponential bomb-¹⁴C decay over 54 yr and by constancy of κ_in to ±0.4% across a 31.6% rise in atmospheric mass).

Claim. The atmospheric expression of mode j in the bomb-¹⁴C response is

δXₐ⁽ʲ⁾(t) = α (wⱼᵀ eₐ) v_{j,a} exp[(λⱼ − λ₁₄) t]

and the atmospheric expression of the same mode in the mass response to an atmospheric emission impulse is

δMₐ⁽ʲ⁾(t) = β (wⱼᵀ eₐ) v_{j,a} exp(λⱼ t)

The atmospheric visibility product (wⱼᵀ eₐ) v_{j,a} is the same in both. Any eigenmode present in the mass impulse response must appear in the bomb-¹⁴C decay with the identical fractional weight, modulo the known ¹⁴C radioactive decay shift λ₁₄ = ln(2)/5730 yr = 1.21 × 10⁻⁴ /yr.

Proof sketch. Decompose eₐ = Σⱼ (wⱼᵀ eₐ) vⱼ. For the bomb-¹⁴C initial condition δX(0) = α eₐ with no subsequent source, solve the perturbation ODE and read off the atmospheric component. For the mass impulse source, the Duhamel solution yields the mass expression. Eigenvectors are shared, so the visibility product is identical. ∎

A sum of exponentials is not an exponential.

For any real μ, distinct real {λ₁, ..., λₙ}, positive d and {c₁, ..., cₙ} with n > 1,

Σₖ cₖ exp(λₖ t) ≢ d exp(μ t)

Proof. Set λ₀ ≔ μ, c₀ ≔ −d, giving Σₖ₌₀ cₖ exp(λₖ t) ≡ 0. Differentiating j times at t = 0 for j = 0, 1, ..., n yields the linear system Vc = 0, where V is the (n+1)×(n+1) Vandermonde matrix with det V = Πᵢ<ₖ(λₖ − λᵢ) ≠ 0. Hence c = V⁻¹0 = 0, contradicting c₀ = −d ≠ 0.

Geometric proof. With wₖ(t) = cₖ exp(−λₖ t) and y = Σwₖ, direct calculation gives (ln y)″ = [Σwₖ · Σλₖ² wₖ − (Σλₖ wₖ)²] / y². By Cauchy–Schwarz, the numerator is > 0, so (ln y)″ > 0. Every sum of exponentials has strictly upward curvature on a semi-log plot; a single exponential has zero curvature (straight line). ∎

The bomb-¹⁴C decay over 1965–2019 is a clean single exponential with τ_rel = 18.383 ± 0.015 yr and R² = 99.998% on the cleaned data. Residual σ₃₂ = 0.539‰ on a 720‰ signal bounds any additional exponential component at approximately one part in 1200.

  1. By the Operator-Equivalence Theorem, the mass impulse response for atmospheric CO₂ shares its eigenmode structure with the ¹⁴C impulse response, mode by mode.
  2. By "A sum of exponentials is not an exponential," any multi-exponential IRF (Bern, or otherwise) cannot equal the observed single-exponential bomb-¹⁴C decay.
  3. Therefore the mass IRF is also a single exponential with essentially the same time constant (modulo λ₁₄, negligible). The Bern IRF and every other multi-exponential CO₂ IRF is structurally excluded.
  4. Furthermore, the physical processes that remove CO₂ from the atmosphere (air-sea gas exchange, photosynthesis, chemical weathering) act on CO₂ molecules, not on isotopic labels. They do not select between ¹²CO₂, ¹³CO₂, and ¹⁴CO₂ at rates comparable to F_out ≈ 230 GtC/yr.
